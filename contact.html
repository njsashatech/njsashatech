Frequently Asked Questions

What is the response time for support requests?

We guarantee rapid response times with SLA commitments: critical issues (under 15 minutes), high priority (under 1 hour), normal priority (under 4 hours), and low priority (under 24 hours). Enterprise clients receive dedicated account managers and priority queue placement.

Do you offer customized solutions?

Yes, we provide fully customized end-to-end solutions tailored to your specific business requirements, industry vertical, technology stack preferences, scalability needs, security requirements, and compliance obligations. Our solutions design process includes detailed requirements analysis, architectural design reviews, and iterative refinement to ensure perfect alignment with your business objectives.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept multiple payment options including international wire transfers, bank transfers, credit cards, and customized payment plans for enterprise clients. We also support flexible billing arrangements including usage-based pricing, fixed-term contracts, and performance-based pricing models for long-term partnerships.

Can we schedule a demo?

Absolutely! We offer personalized product demonstrations tailored to your specific use cases and business scenarios. Our sales team schedules interactive demos showcasing key features, integration capabilities, and ROI metrics. Request a demo by contacting our sales team or use our online scheduling system for immediate availability.

Is there a free trial available?

Yes, we offer 30-day free trials for our mobile management platform and consulting services. Trial accounts include full platform access, dedicated onboarding support, training documentation, and optional consulting sessions to help you maximize value. No credit card required to start your trial.

Do you provide onsite support?

Yes, for enterprise clients, we provide on-site support with dedicated technical teams managing implementation, integration, deployment, ongoing optimization, and 24/7 operational support. Our on-site teams work closely with your internal IT staff to ensure smooth operations and seamless knowledge transfer for long-term self-sufficiency.
