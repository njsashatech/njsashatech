Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 18, 2026
This Privacy Policy applies to all services, products, and applications provided by Nanjing Sasha Technology Co., Ltd

1. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy

Nanjing Sasha Technology Co., Ltd ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website and mobile applications. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information across all our services, applications, and digital platforms.

This policy applies to all applications and services distributed through Google Play Store, Apple App Store, and our official website (njsashatech.com).

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Name, email address, and contact information
  • Business organization name and details
  • Job title and professional information
  • Payment and billing information
  • Account credentials and authentication information
  • Communication preferences

2.2 Technical Information

  • Device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers
  • IP address and location data
  • Browser type, version, and language preferences
  • Mobile carrier and network information
  • Hardware model and memory specifications
  • Application usage patterns and analytics
  • Crash logs and error reports

2.3 Usage & Behavioral Data

  • Features and functions accessed within our applications
  • Time and duration of app usage
  • Search queries and content interactions
  • Download and installation information
  • User interactions and engagement metrics
  • Clickstream data and navigation patterns

2.4 Advertising Data

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) on Android devices
  • Apple IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) on iOS devices
  • Ad impressions and interactions
  • Ad click-through rates
  • Install attribution data

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Providing and improving our services and applications
  • Processing transactions and fulfilling requests
  • Sending technical updates and support notifications
  • Personalizing user experience and recommendations
  • Conducting analytics and performance optimization
  • Fraud detection and security purposes
  • Marketing communications and promotional offers (with consent)
  • Legal compliance and regulatory requirements
  • Resolving disputes and enforcing agreements
  • Research and development of new features

4. Third-Party Data Sharing & Advertising Platforms

4.1 Advertising Partners

Our applications integrate with multiple advertising and monetization platforms to provide free services. Your data may be shared with:

  • Google AdMob: For serving display ads, interstitial ads, banner ads, and rewarded video advertisements
  • Facebook Audience Network: For native ads and banner advertisements
  • AppLovin: For rewarded video ads and programmatic advertising
  • Vungle: For video and rewarded advertising content
  • UnityAds: For mobile game and app advertising
  • Tapjoy: For rewarded video and interactive ads
  • MoPub: For mediated ad network services
  • AdColony: For premium video advertising
  • IronSource: For cross-promotion and rewarded video ads
  • Chartboost: For game and app marketing
  • Inmobi: For native and banner advertising
  • Fyber: For ad monetization and mediation

4.2 Ad Types and Data Collection

  • Banner Ads: Static or rotating ads displayed at top/bottom of app
  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen ads shown during natural breaks in app usage
  • Rewarded Video Ads: Users watch video to earn in-app rewards
  • Native Ads: Ads that match app design and content
  • Splash Ads: Opening screen advertisements

These platforms may collect your device ID, GAID/IDFA, IP address, location data, and browsing history for personalized advertising.

4.3 Other Third Parties

  • Analytics providers (Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel)
  • Cloud service providers and hosting partners
  • Payment processors and financial institutions
  • Customer support and service providers
  • Business partners and integration partners
  • Marketing and email service providers

  • Special protections apply to users under 18 in GDPR/CCPA jurisdictions

6.2 App Store Age Ratings

  • Google Play: Age-appropriate rating system implementation

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
  • End-to-end encryption for sensitive communications
  • Multi-factor authentication for account access
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Secure password storage using industry standards
  • Data encryption at rest in secure data centers
