About Nanjing Sasha Technology

Founded with a vision to deliver cutting-edge technology solutions to enterprises worldwide, Nanjing Sasha Technology Co., Ltd has established itself as a trusted partner for businesses seeking digital transformation and technological excellence. Our commitment to innovation and customer success has made us a leader in enterprise solutions.

Based in Nanjing, China, our company has grown to serve clients across 50+ countries, delivering over 1,000 successful projects with a 98% client satisfaction rate. We employ over 100 professionals with deep expertise in technology development, consulting, and enterprise solutions.

Our success is built on a foundation of talented professionals, cutting-edge technologies, and an unwavering commitment to delivering measurable value to every client we serve.

Our Core Values

Innovation

We continuously push boundaries, embrace cutting-edge technologies, and challenge conventional thinking to create breakthrough solutions that drive industry transformation and deliver exceptional value. Our innovation labs and R&D initiatives ensure we stay ahead of technological trends.

Teamwork

We believe in the power of collaboration and open communication. Through strong teamwork across departments and with our clients, we create synergies that lead to extraordinary results and deliver solutions greater than the sum of individual contributions.

Excellence

We strive for perfection in everything we do. From code quality to project delivery and customer service, our commitment to excellence ensures best-in-class solutions and sets industry standards for quality and reliability.

Integrity

We operate with honesty, transparency, and ethical principles in all our dealings. Trust is fundamental to our relationships with clients, partners, and employees. We hold ourselves accountable to the highest standards of professional conduct.

Customer Focus

We listen carefully to our clients, understand their unique challenges, and deliver solutions that exceed expectations. Your success is our success, and we're committed to building lasting partnerships that create measurable business value.

Continuous Growth

We invest in personal and professional development for all team members. We foster a learning culture through training programs, mentorship, knowledge sharing, and opportunities to grow both technically and professionally throughout your career.
